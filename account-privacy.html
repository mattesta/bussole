Account privacy

Back

Accounts are optional. You can play as a guest without creating an account. Guest games are not imported when you register or sign in.

Sign-in and profile

Firebase Authentication manages email/password and optional Google sign-in. Connecting Google while signed in links that provider to the same Bussole account; Google-only users can add a password. Anonymous guest accounts and earlier guest games are not linked or imported. Google confirmation can replace password reauthentication for sensitive changes. We do not publish the legal name, photo or email supplied by Google. Passwords are not stored in the game's database. Your email is private. Your optional, editable username is used in a multiplayer room only when you enable that preference. Room participants can then see that name.

Detailed statistics on this device

After your email is verified, completed guesses save day, difficulty, solo/multiplayer mode, points and detailed relative, angular and Pro distance errors only in this browser's device storage. These error combinations can reveal a starting position, so they are not uploaded to Firebase or shared with friends. Personal charts and filters use this device's records. They do not sync to another device. Clearing site data, private browsing or browser storage eviction may remove them; use Export my data to keep a copy. Signing out hides account views but keeps this device's records, scoped to the original account. Guest games are not imported.

Deleting statistics clears this browser's records and replays, resets cloud records and rotates the account's statistics generation. Other devices hide their old-generation records on their next account refresh; existing copies on those devices cannot be erased remotely. Anyone who can inspect your browser's storage may access its records.

Minimal cloud records

Firebase stores profile preferences and a reset-generation identifier, personal-best total scores/settings/day, official Daily attempts and totals, optional rankings, friend links and requests, and up to three Pro highlight records with only points, destination name, an opaque replay identifier and the generation. No per-guess error history is uploaded. Pro ties are ordered without sending error values. Project administrators can access this server-side account data. We do not claim that administrators cannot access all personal data or that no possible inference can ever be made from the shared game results.

Old versions uploaded detailed errors. When you open your account in the new version, this browser first saves your current-generation old cloud samples locally, then deletes the cloud source and removes your old shared error snapshot. Failed device storage prevents that automatic source deletion and shows a warning; the project owner's separate legacy purge is still required for accounts that do not migrate. New rules reject further error uploads and block friends from opening legacy snapshots that still contain errors. Installing code or rules alone does not erase all historical copies.

Daily Challenge

Each challenge changes at 00:00 UTC and uses the same five targets and fixed settings for everyone. Guests and repeat plays are unranked. Starting an official attempt with a verified account uses that day's attempt; leaving or reloading does not restart it. We privately store the day, start time, attempt status, random entry key and final score. Completed official scores are shared by day under different random keys, without names, account IDs, email addresses, locations or routes. These are pseudonymized results: the private account record allows us to remove your contribution. Other players can read the daily scores to calculate their comparison. Ties are not counted as players beaten. The percentage changes as more players finish.

The Start official challenge button submits your completed score for this comparison. Delete my statistics and Delete account also remove your daily attempts and shared scores. Data already downloaded to another device cannot be recalled. Official daily scores are client-reported, not certified against cheating.

Your private Daily journey is calculated from your existing completed official daily scores: 0.1 virtual kilometre per point, minus 100 km on the first consecutive missed UTC day, 200 km on the second, and 300 km on the third and later days after your first completion. Completing a Daily resets this absence streak, never below zero. Today is not missed until it ends. No additional location data is stored; a journey snapshot is shared only through the optional social features described below. The journey appears in your export and resets when you delete statistics or your account.

Global error statistics

Community error comparisons and contributions are disabled. The app does not submit detailed errors or request pooled community statistics. The optional Daily kilometre leaderboard is a separate score-based feature.

Friends and rankings

Saving a verified username, creating a friend link, sending or accepting a request, or opting into the global ranking enables a small display profile containing your chosen username, or Explorer if none is set. Merely registering or playing does not publish that profile. Usernames are unique regardless of capitalisation. Saving one reserves it and lets verified players find your display profile by its exact username to send a friend request. Changing it releases the old name; account deletion releases the current name. Existing unreserved names must be saved before username lookup works. Signed-in verified players who know a player ID or invite link can also read that display name; there is no browsable profile directory or email search. A friend link contains your Bussole account identifier. Share it with people you know.

Sending a friend request and the other player accepting it grants mutual access to record snapshots: personal-best scores/settings and your Daily journey. Pending requests grant no access. Friends receive no per-guess error samples, starting positions, exact headings, chosen distances or routes. Pro highlights share only points and destination names. Friendship does not expose your email or profile preferences. Either participant can cancel, decline or remove the relationship. Removal blocks future reads and hides open friend views in this app; copies already downloaded cannot be recalled.

The global kilometre leaderboard is a separate, optional setting, off by default. Opting in publishes your username, account ID, virtual balance, snapshot day and last completed Daily day so missed-day penalties can be applied while you are offline. Your email and statistical errors are not public. Friends can compare their shared journeys without opting into the global board. Balances and shared statistics sync when players use the app; this is not a certified competition or an anti-cheat system. Snapshots are refreshed from private records and protected against stale writes after statistics deletion.

Your controls

You can export your data, delete all personal statistics while keeping the account, or delete your account and personal statistics. Deleting statistics also removes your shared records and global leaderboard entry, switches global ranking participation off, and resets the Daily journey. Friend relationships remain until removed or the account is deleted. Global error comparisons are disabled. Already shared room results are not removed from other players' current match views by deleting your account history.

This page describes the account prototype's data handling. The only public account data is the limited display profile and, when you opt in, the kilometre leaderboard. Email search is not provided; verified players can look up an exact reserved username. In-app account deletion removes the private data, social snapshots, display profile, leaderboard entry and known friendship/request edges before deleting the Authentication user.

Home

Location and Pro replays

The current game calculates guesses on your device. Starting GPS coordinates, exact heading, chosen distance and replay routes are not uploaded as readable game records. Solo geometry stays on the device. Private room participants exchange encrypted lines as described below. Your top three replayable Pro shots are stored only in this browser, scoped to your account. They do not sync to another device and may disappear if you clear browser data or use private browsing. Anyone able to use your signed-in browser or inspect its storage may access them. Signing out hides account views but keeps your local highlights for your next sign-in. Delete statistics or Delete account removes this browser's highlights; copies on another device cannot be erased remotely. Old generations are hidden when you sign in again.

Firebase retains score records and the points and destination names of your best shots. Accepted friends see only those points and destination names for highlights. Project administrators can access server-side account information, scores and destination names. We do not claim that administrators have no access to all personal data. Your export includes locally available replay geometry.

Multiplayer room retention

Public rooms share scores and the chosen target only. Private rooms also show each participating player’s starting location and line after the round is revealed. Joining a private room shares this information with its participants; a private room code is an invitation, not proof of friendship. Invite only people you trust. The browser encrypts a separate copy for each recipient. Firebase stores only public exchange keys and encrypted line messages; the secret keys remain in the room tab’s device storage. Reading database values alone does not decrypt these messages. Someone participating in the room can see and retain the shared locations, including the project owner if they join. This does not protect against a compromised device or an operator actively modifying the website or key exchange. Received private lines are used for the current globe view; they are not added to your account statistics, friend records or cloud Pro highlights. Encrypted messages are deleted when the host advances to the next round; room expiration and cleanup also apply. Device exchange keys are scoped to the account and room, expire after 24 hours and are removed when you explicitly leave. Your own guesses stay in this tab for a personal match summary, for up to 24 hours and until you leave the room. A match summary can remain in tab storage until you close the tab or sign out. Other players' scores are client-reported. The last player to leave normally deletes the room after checking the current player list. New matches clear previous match results and target history. Rooms have a maximum lifetime of 24 hours; access is blocked after expiry. Physical removal of abandoned rooms uses a separately configured hourly cleanup job and can be delayed or unavailable if that job is not running.

Earlier app versions uploaded location and replay data. The new rules block further uploads and legacy-room access, but installing new code or rules does not itself erase existing records. Legacy rooms and cloud replay geometry must be removed by the project owner using the migration procedure. Opening your account also migrates your own old highlights into this browser and removes their cloud geometry. Once the owner has purged old cloud copies, they cannot be recovered on other devices. Infrastructure providers may process connection metadata such as IP addresses; map and search requests are separate from Firebase game records.

Optional Daily reminders

Daily reminders are off by default and require permission on each device. If enabled, we store a private Web Push subscription, browser-generated subscription keys, a random device/account binding, time zone and last update time, plus a small journey snapshot and delivery state. The scoped reminder sender uses only this data, not locations, targets or private replay geometry. Project administrators can access stored reminder data. GitHub Actions schedules free, best-effort deliveries around 17:00 in the device’s saved time zone; delivery can be late or missed. Push providers deliver the notification, and its content may show your world progress on the lock screen. You can turn off reminders or sign out to unsubscribe this device. Account deletion removes reminder data; statistics deletion resets the progress snapshot. The sender removes expired endpoints and registrations not refreshed for 90 days during its due runs. If scheduling is disabled, this cleanup also stops.
