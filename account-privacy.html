Account privacy

Back

Accounts are optional. You can play as a guest without creating an account. Guest games are not imported when you register or sign in.

Sign-in and profile

Firebase Authentication manages your email and password. Passwords are not stored in the game's database. Your email is private. Your optional, editable username is used in a multiplayer room only when you enable that preference. Room participants can then see that name.

Personal statistics

After your email is verified, completed guesses can save the difficulty, single-player or multiplayer mode, day, score, relative final error, signed angular error and, in Pro mode, signed relative distance error. Your account history contains no starting coordinates, target coordinates or names, routes, exact headings, room codes or round numbers. A random or hashed record key prevents duplicate saves; an internal generation key prevents old open games from restoring deleted statistics.

Statistics stay private to your account. Day and difficulty filters let you compare periods. Guess charts cannot be linked back to a place or a particular round through the account history. The live game's temporary room data and the current match summary are separate; participants in private rooms can still inspect shared routes during the match.

Completed five-round single-player games can also save a personal-best score, day and settings. Practice does not set game records. Personal bests are included in exports and removed when you delete your statistics or account.

Optional community comparison

Contributing is off by default and requires separate consent. If enabled, the server uses your saved error values to calculate pooled comparisons. Contributions remain technically linked to your account so that they can be removed. This is pseudonymized processing, not a promise of complete anonymity. Other players cannot read your individual records or profile.

Only pooled means and counts are published, and only when at least five accounts contribute to the selected difficulty. The pooled comparison is all-time and may reflect different experience levels and settings. Turning off consent removes your past and future contributions when the server refreshes. Enabling it includes your existing saved statistics as well as future results.

Your controls

You can export your data, delete all personal statistics while keeping the account, or delete your account and personal statistics. Deleting statistics also switches community contributions off. Aggregate comparisons refresh after removal. Already shared room results are not removed from other players' current match views by deleting your account history.

This page describes the account prototype's data handling. No public profiles, account search or global leaderboard are provided.
